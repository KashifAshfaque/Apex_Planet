# ApexPlanet Task 2: EDA & Business Intelligence

## Project Overview

This project focuses on Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) and Business Intelligence using a sales dataset.

The main objective was to identify patterns, trends and relationships in the data and answer business questions using SQL.

## Dataset Overview

- Total rows: 1000
- Total columns: 12
- Unique orders: 992
- Numerical variables: Age, Quantity, Unit_Price, Total_Sales
- Categorical variables: Gender, City, Product, Category

## Work Performed

### 1. Descriptive Statistics & Univariate Analysis

- Calculated summary statistics using Pandas.
- Analyzed numerical variables.
- Analyzed categorical variables.
- Created histograms for numerical variables.
- Created bar charts for categorical variables.

### 2. SQL Business Questions

SQL was used to answer the following business questions:

1. What is the total sales generated by each product?
2. Which are the top 5 products based on total sales?
3. Which cities generate the highest total sales?
4. How do total sales change month by month?
5. Which product categories generate the highest total sales?
6. What is the average order value for each product category?
7. How does total sales differ between male and female customers?

### 3. Multivariate Analysis

The following relationships were analyzed:

- Quantity vs Total Sales
- Unit Price vs Total Sales
- Age vs Total Sales

### 4. Correlation Analysis

A correlation matrix and heatmap were created for:

- Age
- Quantity
- Unit Price
- Total Sales

Important correlations observed:

- Quantity and Total Sales: approximately 0.65
- Unit Price and Total Sales: approximately 0.69
- Age and Total Sales: approximately 0.00

### 5. Dashboard

A static sales dashboard was created using the insights from the analysis.

The dashboard includes:

- Revenue by Product
- Revenue by Category
- Monthly Sales Trend
- Revenue by Gender
- Key Performance Indicators

## Key Insights

- Laptop generated the highest total revenue among the products.
- Patna recorded the highest total revenue among the cities.
- Electronics generated the highest revenue among the product categories.
- Grocery had the highest average order value among the categories.
- Male customers generated higher total revenue than female customers in this dataset.
- Quantity and Unit Price both show positive relationships with Total Sales.
- Age has almost no linear relationship with Total Sales.
- Monthly revenue fluctuates across the dataset period.
